DATEV -
Banking software


Design Ideation


This project explores how banking tools can better support small to medium-sized craft businesses. The focus was on improving clarity and efficiency in understanding financial transactions and making it easier for both business owners and staff to access the information they need. The goal was to design solutions that enable quick insights into company liquidity and simplify the process of finding specific transaction details.
